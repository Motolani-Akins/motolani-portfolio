The Story

A writer, first.
Everything else serves that.

I am Motolani Deborah, a Nigerian writer with a law degree from Obafemi Awolowo University. I have been writing professionally since 2021.

My work sits in three places. UX writing and research, where I shape the words inside a product and do the research that makes those words right. Content writing, where I write the articles and brand stories that make people stop and actually read. And technical writing, where I take a complicated process, policy, or system and turn it into something a normal person can follow.

The law degree is not decoration. It is why I am comfortable in the rooms most writers avoid: compliance, policy, regulation, the dense and the exacting. I read closely, I write plainly, and I own the result from the first draft to the final line.

I keep my fiction separate, under a pen name. This is the professional writer, and this is the work I stake my name on.
